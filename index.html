      The training vs tuning ladder

      Climb only when the rung below measurably fails on your eval set.

      RungMoveReach for it when
      1Better promptsAlways start here. Most quality problems are instruction problems.
      2Few-shot examplesOutput shape or tone is inconsistent and examples fix it.
      3RAG (retrieval)The model needs your facts, and facts change.
      4Fine-tuningBehaviour must be consistent at scale and prompts plateau.
      5Training from scratchAlmost never for product teams. The product is the model itself.
