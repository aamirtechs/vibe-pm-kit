# Launch and grow

A vibe-built product launches the same way any product does: to real
users, in a place they already gather, with proof it works. This page is
the kit's honest playbook. Nothing here guarantees reach; everything here
is what the projects that do spread have in common.

## Before launch
- Finish templates/launch-checklist.md without skipping the AI behaviour
  and cost lines. AI products fail publicly in those two places.
- Prepare the demo asset: one short clip or screenshot sequence showing
  the payoff in under 30 seconds. Settings screens do not spread; results
  do.
- Write the launch post in the user's words, from real complaints you
  solved, not from your feature list.

## Launch where the users are
- One community at a time, following its self-promotion rules, with the
  maker present to answer questions for the first hours
- A write-up of what you built and what the evals taught you travels
  further than a feature announcement
- Ask early users for the sentence they would tell a friend, then use
  their sentence, not yours

## After launch
- Answer every issue and comment fast in the first weeks; responsiveness
  is the growth strategy nobody can copy overnight
- Ship the fixes users asked for, visibly, and credit the reporters
- Convert recurring questions into docs, templates, and eval cases, so
  the product compounds instead of just accumulating

## What not to do
- No fake stars, bought upvotes, or engagement pods: platforms detect
  them, and the penalty lands on the account, not the post
- No spam posting the same link across communities that ban it
- No inflated claims: numbers you cannot show will be checked publicly

Growth that lasts is a loop: useful thing, honest proof, fast response,
another useful thing.
