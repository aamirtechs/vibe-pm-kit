# PRD: <product name>

## Summary
Two or three sentences: what it is, who it is for, why now.

## Problem and evidence
The user problem, with the strongest evidence you have (interviews, tickets,
analytics, your own repeated pain). Link sources, do not paste private data.

## Users and jobs to be done
- Primary user: <role> trying to <job> so that <outcome>
- Secondary user:

## Solution outline
The shape of the solution in plain words. No code, no stack choices yet,
unless a constraint forces one.

## Scope for v1
The smallest end-to-end slice a real user can complete. List the screens,
actions, and data it touches.

## Out of scope for v1
Everything tempting that waits. Be explicit; this list protects the build.

## Build notes for the AI builder
- Stack and libraries that are allowed or banned
- Existing code, files, or designs to reuse
- Conventions to follow (naming, folders, test style)

## Acceptance criteria
Observable, testable statements. If a criterion cannot be checked by a
person in five minutes, rewrite it.

## Metrics and rollout
Launch metric, guardrail metric, who sees it first, and the rollback plan.

## Open questions
Decisions still owed, who owns each, and the default if nobody answers.
